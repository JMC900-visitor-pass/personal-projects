# BookForge and Prompt-Generation Tools

Coordinate repeatable local AI writing and visual-prompt workflows.

## Implemented work

Staged concept, character, drafting, critique, and editing calls; chapter/state checkpoints and resume; shorter guided writing flow; streamed card-art prompt generation with configurable styles, creative/strict modes, and history.

## Status and limitations

AI workflow prototypes. Generated writing still needs human editing. BookForge's “agents” are programmed model roles and stages; this does not establish independently trained models or a team of autonomous systems.

## Source snapshot

3 selected text source files are included. These are portfolio snapshots, not a guaranteed complete runnable distribution. Personal notes, original documentation, local databases, saves, credentials, generated content, binaries, and artwork are excluded. Local user paths and non-example email addresses are replaced in the copies. Runtime defaults may therefore need configuration.

External libraries, models, datasets, and upstream projects remain credited to their authors. No blanket license or claim of sole authorship is made for this collection.

## Architecture and design decisions

Staged model calls separate outline, drafting, critique, and editing tasks. Checkpoints support interruption and resume. Programmed model roles are not independently trained models.
