# Procedural Sprites and Small Browser Games

Explore procedural visuals, persistent game loops, and compact interfaces.

## Implemented work

Procedural creature and plant sprites, palette and silhouette rules, and contact sheets; browser creature-game systems with combat/progression and save/load; AI and portrait connection functions in Datakeeper.

## Status and limitations

Creative prototypes. Source integration functions do not by themselves prove that every external service connection works.

## Source snapshot

4 selected text source files are included. These are portfolio snapshots, not a guaranteed complete runnable distribution. Personal notes, original documentation, local databases, saves, credentials, generated content, binaries, and artwork are excluded. Local user paths and non-example email addresses are replaced in the copies. Runtime defaults may therefore need configuration.

External libraries, models, datasets, and upstream projects remain credited to their authors. No blanket license or claim of sole authorship is made for this collection.

## Architecture and design decisions

Procedural shape and palette rules provide repeatable visual variation. Browser prototypes combine persistence with creature progression. External service connections require their own verification.
