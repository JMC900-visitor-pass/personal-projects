# Browser Audio and Noise Tools

Create adjustable ambient audio with live controls and exports.

## Implemented work

Browser audio graphs; procedural noise; filters, oscillators, and modulation; layered controls and presets; live visualisation; WAV/MP3 export functions.

## Status and limitations

Browser audio prototypes. Export behaviour and audible quality were not tested during this review. No medical, therapeutic, or calibrated acoustic claims are supported.

## Source snapshot

2 selected text source files are included. These are portfolio snapshots, not a guaranteed complete runnable distribution. Personal notes, original documentation, local databases, saves, credentials, generated content, binaries, and artwork are excluded. Local user paths and non-example email addresses are replaced in the copies. Runtime defaults may therefore need configuration.

External libraries, models, datasets, and upstream projects remain credited to their authors. No blanket license or claim of sole authorship is made for this collection.

## Architecture and design decisions

Web Audio nodes form a graph of sources, filters, gain, and modulation. Browser rendering supplies interactive controls and visualisation. Export and listening quality need independent verification.
